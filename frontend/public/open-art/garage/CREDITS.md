# Workshop prop credits

Optimized models used in the Avensky garage. Each model also carries its attribution and modification notes in the glTF asset metadata.

- **Garage Door** by FrodoUndead. Reoriented and resized the shutter, packed its surface details, and resized and compressed the original textures. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/garage-door-d5c417514e834a04b27b1a013d86a805) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Low-Poly Metal Shelf** by Ronstu. Reoriented and grounded the shelf, compressed the original surface maps, removed unused UVs and corrected the metallic texture assignment. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-metal-shelf-d9f0169a2a254d5a8668d4cf598c743e) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Metal Tool Chest** by John Hutcheson (model and textures), Yann Kervran (rigging). Baked a static open-lid pose, normalized orientation and scale, reduced geometry and compressed and packed the original surface maps. [Original model](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_tool_chest) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Dirty Old Tires** by chrisg4919. Selected one tire, stood it upright, normalized its scale, and resized and compressed the original surface maps. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dirty-old-tires-3ac31ac8a2cf4b7187b4d1b95d2aeea0) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
